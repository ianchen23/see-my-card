No cards showing? Check what's saved to your Google account in Google Wallet.

On a computer, you can also paste this into Chrome's address bar: chrome://settings/payments

Is this safe?
  • No tracking, ads, accounts, or server. The page is a single file and runs no code.
  • Chrome fills the boxes on your own device; the page has no way to send them anywhere. Closing the tab erases everything.
  • Verify it: right-click → View page source and compare with the source on GitHub.
  • Rather not trust any website? Host your own copy for free.
Recommended: lock your cards

In Chrome, go to Settings → Autofill and passwords → Payment methods and turn on Verify it's you to autofill payment methods. Then nobody using your unlocked device can see your cards without your fingerprint, face, or PIN.
